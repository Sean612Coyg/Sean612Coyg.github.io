# Stepper Motor With Sean Wei and Wachi Manwichachai

## Materials
**Arduino Uno**: ATmega328P Microcontroller Board | 1 | Executes stepper control code and sends positioning signals. |

**Stepper Motor**: 28BYJ-48 (5V DC Unipolar) | 1 | Provides precise angular rotation ($\theta$) for the scanning platform. |

**Stepper Motor Driver**: ULN2003 Driver Board | 1 | Amplifies signals from Arduino pins to drive the high-current stepper coils. |

**Breadboard**: Solderless Breadboard (830 Point) | 1 | Allows rapid, non-permanent circuit wiring and power rail distribution. |

**External Battery / Power Supply**: 5V–9V Power Source (or 4x AA Battery Pack) | 1 | Supplies isolated, dedicated power to the stepper motor to prevent microcontroller brownouts. |

**Jumper Wires**: Male-to-Male & Male-to-Female | Assorted | Connects microcontroller digital pins, driver board, and power rails. |
